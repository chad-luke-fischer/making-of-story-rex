# The Making of story-rex

How **story-rex** came to exist: a skill that takes recorded conversations and weaves them into literary stories in an Obsidian vault, linked and tagged, with every quote checked against the recording before a story counts as done.

The page tells its origin in the same arc the vault tells about itself: the tasting that started it, the pitch, a look inside `day_watcher`, and what it can become.

**Live:** https://chad-luke-fischer.github.io/making-of-story-rex/

A single self-contained `index.html`, with no build step and no libraries. The low-poly book in the hero is drawn by hand on a 2D canvas. Styled in the violet skin of [wiki-dispatcher](https://chad-luke-fischer.github.io/wiki-dispatcher/).
